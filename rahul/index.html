PRODUCT APPROACH

Less feature-first. More problem-first.

I like understanding the situation before deciding what to build. The goal is not to add complexity — it is to make the next step clearer for the person using the product.

01
Frame the problem

Who is struggling, and what are they trying to achieve?

02
Map the workflow

What happens today, where does friction appear, and what matters most?

03
Choose the smallest useful solution

Define the user journey, MVP scope and trade-offs before expanding.

04
Build and learn

Turn the plan into something inspectable, then improve it with evidence.

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Business context meets hands-on building.

I’m Rahul Kumar, an early-career product builder and founder of Atelier OG. I’m pursuing a BCA while working as a Retail Gemologist at Tanishq, part of Titan Company Limited.

Retail has given me a close view of customer questions, product explanation and day-to-day operational realities. My independent projects let me explore how those kinds of problems can be translated into digital workflows and software products.

I’m especially interested in AI-enabled products, SaaS, workflow design and zero-to-one product development — from understanding the need to shaping and building an initial solution.
